What the Sector Asks of Its Leaders

Scale, safety and succession — at once.

Qatar's energy economy is entering a new chapter. The North Field expansion — among the largest LNG capacity programmes in the world — together with downstream, petrochemical and industrial diversification, is raising the demand for leaders who can run assets of global significance while building the next generation of national talent.

Those leaders are scarce, and the best of them are not looking. They are found through original research across the producing nations, the international oil companies, the engineering contractors and the national champions — and assessed for the judgement that matters most in a high-hazard environment, not merely for a record of tenure.

Each appointment also carries a Qatarisation dimension. We treat it as a question of capability and succession rather than compliance: who leads today, who will lead next, and how the two are connected.

Our Approach

How we conduct an energy mandate.

Industrial appointments fail less often on competence than on fit — with the asset, the partners, the governance and the culture of safety. Our method is built around that reality.

  • A technical and governance brief written with the board, the shareholders and, where relevant, the joint-venture partners
  • Original research across producing nations, international operators, contractors and service companies
  • Assessment of safety leadership, operating judgement and the ability to develop national talent
  • Discreet referencing with former peers, partners and board members
  • Engagement through onboarding, so the appointment holds through the first operating cycle
Questions

What boards often ask us.

Do you conduct executive search for Qatar's energy and LNG sector?

Yes. Alides conducts retained, partner-led search from Doha for chief executives, operations, finance, major-project, HSE and board appointments across energy, LNG, petrochemical and industrial organisations, and for the companies that serve them.

Can you search internationally for an industrial leader based in Qatar?

Yes. Through ECI Group, the international executive search network of which Alides is a member, we research and approach leaders across producing nations, international operators and engineering contractors, while the mandate is led personally from Doha.

How do you take Qatarisation into account in industrial appointments?

We treat it as a succession question: every senior appointment is considered together with the development of Qatari leaders who can succeed or work alongside the appointee.
