What the Sector Asks of Its Leaders

Growth, control and credibility.

Qatar's financial sector is broadening — through the development of capital markets, the growth of asset and wealth management, digital and Islamic finance, and the international firms establishing themselves at the Qatar Financial Centre. Each of these shifts raises the bar for leadership.

Regulated roles bring a second test. Senior appointments in banks, insurers and QFC-licensed firms are subject to fitness and propriety expectations, and boards of listed companies operate under the governance code of the Qatar Financial Markets Authority. An appointment must therefore be defensible before it is announced.

The candidates who meet that standard are few, internationally mobile and rarely in the market. They are reached through original research and approached with discretion.

Our Approach

How we conduct a regulated appointment.

We design each search so that the board can appoint with confidence — and explain its choice to a regulator, to shareholders and to the market.

  • A brief that integrates the strategy, the risk appetite and the regulatory requirements of the role
  • Research across regional and international banks, asset managers, insurers and regulators
  • Assessment of integrity, risk judgement and the capacity to lead under scrutiny
  • Thorough referencing and background verification before any recommendation
  • Support through the approval process and onboarding, so the appointment holds
Questions

What boards often ask us.

Does Alides conduct executive search for banks and financial institutions in Qatar?

Yes. Alides conducts retained, partner-led executive search from Doha for banks, investment and asset managers, insurers, Islamic finance institutions and QFC-licensed firms — for chief executives, finance, risk, compliance, investment and board roles.

Do you take regulatory approval into account?

Yes. For regulated roles we build the requirements of the regulator into the brief, the assessment and the referencing, so that the recommended candidate can be submitted for approval with confidence.

Can you search for independent directors for a listed company in Qatar?

Yes. We conduct independent and non-executive director searches and advise on board composition with reference to the governance expectations that apply to listed companies.
