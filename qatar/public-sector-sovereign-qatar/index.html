What These Institutions Ask of Their Leaders

Two mandates, one leader.

Qatar National Vision 2030 and the Third National Development Strategy set clear ambitions for a diversified, knowledge-based economy. Government-related entities and public investment platforms are central to that ambition — as investors, as operators and as builders of national capability.

Their leaders must perform commercially while answering to a public shareholder; build world-class organisations while developing Qatari leadership; and hold the confidence of ministries, boards and international partners alike. The profile is rare, and it cannot be found in a database.

Board composition matters as much as executive appointments. We advise on the independence, skills and committee structure that allow a board to oversee a strategic institution with authority.

Our Approach

How we conduct a public-interest mandate.

Appointments in public institutions are scrutinised long after they are made. Our process is designed to produce a decision that can be explained, defended and sustained.

  • A brief that sets the institutional mandate alongside the commercial objectives
  • Research across sovereign funds, public institutions, global investors and the private sector
  • Assessment of judgement, integrity and the capacity to lead in a multi-stakeholder governance
  • Documented recommendations that a board or shareholder can stand behind
  • Absolute confidentiality from the first conversation, and engagement through onboarding
Questions

What boards often ask us.

Does Alides advise government-related entities in Qatar?

Yes. Alides conducts retained executive search and board advisory from Doha for government-related entities, public investment platforms and national institutions — for chief executives, investment and finance leaders, and board and committee members.

Do you conduct board searches for public institutions?

Yes. We conduct independent director and committee-chair searches, and advise on board composition and skills, in line with the governance expectations that apply to the institution.

How do you protect confidentiality in sensitive public appointments?

Each mandate is exclusive and led personally by a partner. Candidates are approached individually and in confidence; nothing is circulated or advertised.
